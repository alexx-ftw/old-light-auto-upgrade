# Independent review: 1.17.8 local performance sampling

Reviewed on 2026-10-15 against base commit `4393d792984c1a8cfe84dd2c4bdc74a50de6b8bb` (1.17.7).

## Outcome

No blocking findings remain in the reviewed candidate. The reviewer changed only `tests/metrics-timing-review.test.cjs` and this report; production fixes were made by the implementation owner and then independently retested.

This is a new optional diagnostic, not evidence that the user's actual browser is slow. A direct execution of the base production wrapper with local metrics enabled and no remote collector returned `startTiming() === null` and zero timing counts. The candidate records fixed local duration categories under that existing opt-in. The base therefore lacks the intended feature; the review findings below were issues in the new candidate, not newly discovered defects in the shipped 1.17.7 timer.

## Findings resolved during review

1. **Consent-generation race.** The initial finish path validated the captured consent and then called ordinary `record()`, which could read a newer generation. A deterministic cross-tab interleave demonstrated an old-generation sample being recorded into the next generation. Final recording now passes the captured permission to `recordAttempt()`, including retry validation. Same-tab opt-out, clear, re-enable, another tab's revocation/rotation, and rotation between validation and recording are covered.
2. **Remote measurement included local collection overhead.** The initial wrapper calculated the remote end after local persistence and panel refresh. A 10 ms step plus a simulated 390 ms refresh reported 400 ms. It now captures remote duration before local collection/refresh. The existing remote collector remains independently gated and its vocabulary remains exactly 36 events; the five new local bins are excluded.
3. **Sampling failures left the panel stale.** The initial wrapper refreshed only after a successful local write. A failed write silently stopped collection without updating the existing status panel. It now refreshes after an attempted local token, including failure. A sampled consent-read failure before token creation also uses a guarded notification callback. Ten thousand subsequent blocked ticks do not repeatedly notify or retry storage.
4. **Clock rollback bypassed the sampling gate.** A backwards wall-clock adjustment initially permitted an immediate additional probe. The gate now re-anchors and skips the probe, requiring a full minute from the adjusted anchor. Ten thousand decreasing clock readings cause no extra storage or performance-clock reads. Sampling remains a diagnostic governed by the scheduling clock, not a high-precision rate limiter.
5. **Local measurement included its own end-consent read.** The end clock is now captured before permission-validation storage access. A 10 ms step with 100 ms simulated consent-read latency stays in the under-16-ms category.

## Independent regression coverage

`tests/metrics-timing-review.test.cjs` contains 15 tests covering:

- Default-off behavior with no writes or performance-clock reads and one consent probe across 10,000 ticks.
- Opaque, frozen, identity-bound, single-use tokens; old tokens cannot consume a replacement sample.
- Every exact bucket boundary and the 60-second duration limit.
- Invalid/throwing clocks without propagation into the scheduler.
- No exact sample times, durations, or consent generations in report snapshots.
- Consent transitions and the read/record interleave described above.
- One-minute gating, rollback recovery, and bounded repeated-tick work.
- Local-only collection, unchanged remote timing cadence, and no forwarding of local bins.
- Immediate status refresh on failed sampled reads/writes.
- Exclusion of collection/refresh work from measured end times.

The existing frozen-vocabulary/storage test was reviewed as part of the change: local events grow from 36 to 41, and a full 30-day maximum-counter snapshot grows from 33,676 to 38,836 bytes, below the unchanged 65,536-byte read limit. There is still only one pending timing sample and no new unbounded queue.

## Limits and release gate

This review is offline. No real Brave/Violentmonkey session, Drive login or synchronization, live game action, network timing, FPS, or actual user-session performance was tested. The updater's existing 19 tests also pass, but real installer permissions and behavior remain unverified.

The samples cover selected synchronous helper steps only and may miss isolated spikes. Existing local counter contention remains approximate. This review does not replace the complete final `validate-release.cjs` gate, clean-tree integrity checks, or verification of the published commit; the implementation owner records those separately.
